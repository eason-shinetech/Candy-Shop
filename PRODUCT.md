# PRODUCT.md — Candy Shop

Impeccable / design-agent context. Gameplay rules live in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-24-candy-shop-design.md`. Visual lock: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-24-candy-shop-art-bible.md`.

## Surface

**Product UI** (mobile game HUD + menus), not a marketing landing page.

## Audience

Kids and casual players on **Android**, portrait phone. One-hand thumb reach. UI in **Simplified Chinese and English**.

## Product lane

Cute candy shop management: tap a 3D pile to fill customer orders. Fast, readable, sweet. Feels like a toy bakery, not a fintech dashboard.

## Voice

Short, warm, slightly playful. Buttons are verbs. **zh:** 开始营业, 领取, 购买. **en:** Open Shop, Claim, Buy. No corporate tone. No meme slang. Same keys, both locales — see `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-24-candy-shop-i18n.md`.

## Anti-references

Generic AI SaaS (Inter, purple glow, nested cards). Photoreal candy ads. Horror/dark UI. Landscape tablet dashboards. Thin-line “luxury fashion” UI.

## Native

Unity **6000.0.77f1**, uGUI + TextMeshPro, 1080×1920, Android only.
